RSSI Value,Signal Quality,What to Expect
-30 dBm to -50 dBm,Excellent,Devices are in the same room or very close.
-50 dBm to -80 dBm,Very Good,"Stable connection, ideal for indoor environments with walls."
-80 dBm to -100 dBm,Good / Medium,Typical medium-range signal (a few blocks away).
-100 dBm to -120 dBm,Weak,Approaching limit. Packet error rate may increase.
-120 dBm to -140 dBm,Critical Limit,"Where LoRa excels. Other wireless technologies would drop, but LoRa still receives (especially with higher SF)."
